Privacy Policy — CapyTalk

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapyTalk ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application for learning Thai.

1. No Account, No Login

The App does not require you to create an account or log in. You can use the App anonymously.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Only On Your Device
The App stores your learning progress, streaks, XP, spaced-repetition (SRS) schedule, and app settings (language, theme, notification preferences) locally on your device, using your device's local storage. We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to our servers. Uninstalling the App or using the "Delete all my data" option in Settings permanently erases it.

2.2 Purchases and Subscriptions
If you purchase a Premium subscription, the purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier tied to your device/store account and your purchase/subscription status (active, expired, trial, etc.) — it does not receive your name, email, or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on how they process this data.

2.3 Crash Reports (if enabled)
We may use a crash-reporting service (Sentry) to help us find and fix bugs. If enabled, this collects technical diagnostic information such as device model, OS version, and the app state at the time of a crash. This data is not linked to your identity.

2.4 We Do Not Collect
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and the App does not contain third-party advertising.

3. How We Use Information

Information described above is used solely to: operate core App features (progress tracking, reminders, spaced repetition), process and validate subscriptions, and diagnose and fix technical issues.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share data only with the service providers named above (Apple/Google for purchases, RevenueCat for subscription status, and, if enabled, Sentry for crash diagnostics), solely to provide the App's functionality.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete the App or use "Delete all my data" in Settings, which immediately and permanently erases it. Because we do not operate user accounts or servers that store your personal data, there is no separate account-deletion process — deleting local data (or the App itself) is complete removal. If you have questions about data held by RevenueCat or our crash-reporting provider, contact us using the details below and we will assist you or direct you to the relevant provider's deletion process.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data associated with you. Since the App does not maintain server-side personal data or accounts, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device via the "Delete all my data" option. For any other requests, contact us below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
